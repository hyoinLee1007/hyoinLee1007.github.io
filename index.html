Web Video-Based Robot Behavior Data Generation

GIST AI Lab · Undergraduate Researcher · Jun. 2026 – Sep. 2026

Explored a pipeline for generating robot manipulation trajectories from human videos. Reconstructed hand–object motion and investigated contact-aware optimization to refine interactions. Analyzed how contact-attraction and other objective terms contributed across trajectory chunks, while identifying limitations from reconstruction and contact-estimation errors.

Admittance-Guided Imitation Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation

ICROS 2026 · Undergraduate Researcher · Dec. 2025 – Jun. 2026

Developed an admittance-guided imitation learning framework for contact-rich wiping. Estimated external forces from motor currents without an external force/torque sensor and used admittance control to improve compliance during demonstration collection and execution.

Imitation Learning · Admittance Control · MuJoCo · Force Estimation
